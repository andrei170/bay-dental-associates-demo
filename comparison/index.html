34 years on Finger Road. Independent and partner-owned. A team measured in decades. And invisible to AI search.

Bay Dental Associates has provided family dentistry to the greater Green Bay area from its Finger Road office since 1992, with roots back to 1972. It stayed independent and partner-owned while corporate chains moved in around it, and it kept a team that stays 20, 30, and 40 years. Every one of those facts is a trust signal AI search engines reward. None of them are in the website's structured data, and the practice is being lost next to the much larger "Dental Associates" group. This document is the audit and the surgical fix list.

Bay Dental Associates: before vs after

Same practice. Same Finger Road address. Same independent, partner-owned family dentistry. Two very different chances of being the dentist a Green Bay family picks when they finally search for one tonight.

What's there now

The live site states the facts in prose. It says "family dentistry for over 25 years," it names Dr. Roznik, Dr. Nockerts, and Dr. Jensen, it mentions team members with decades of tenure. But the prose is all an AI engine has to work with, because the structured-data layer underneath it is not built.

  • No Person schema for any of the partner-dentists or current dentists
  • No Dentist / MedicalBusiness / LocalBusiness schema with hours, services, and address
  • The long-tenured team, a real trust signal, sits in prose, unmarked and uncaptured
  • No FAQPage schema, the format AI engines lift verbatim into answers
  • No dedicated heritage page defending the 1992 Finger Road and 1972 founding story
  • No identity content distinguishing Bay Dental from the much larger "Dental Associates" group
  • The independent, partner-owned advantage is never positioned against corporate dental chains

34 years of trust. Zero of it is machine-readable.

We tested five buyer queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews: family dentist Green Bay, dentist near me Green Bay, dentist east Green Bay, independent dentist Green Bay, and best dentist Green Bay WI. Bay Dental Associates surfaced in 0 of 5.

The answers went to the large "Dental Associates" Wisconsin group, to Aspen Dental and Midwest Dental, and to a handful of independents like Oral Health Associates and Wagner Family Dentistry. A 34-year independent practice with a team measured in decades is being out-cited by corporate chains and by a group it gets confused with.

The fix is not a rebuild of the practice. It is the schema, the heritage authority page, the long-tenured-team content, the name-distinction identity work, and the independent-vs-chain positioning. The hard part, the 34 years and the team that stays and the partner ownership, is already done.

The 90-day plan.
