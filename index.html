Our Heritage

A Green Bay practice with roots that go back further than the building.

Bay Dental Associates traces to 1972, when the practice opened in downtown Green Bay. It took the Bay Dental name as Dr. Thomas J. Nockerts joined in 1981 and Dr. Richard Jensen joined in 1983, the partners who shaped it into the family practice Green Bay knows today.

In 1992, the practice settled into its current office at 2460 Finger Road on Green Bay's east side. It has provided family dentistry to the greater Green Bay area from that address ever since, more than three decades in one place, caring for the same neighborhoods and, in many cases, the same families across generations.

"Some of our patients first sat in this chair as children. Now they bring their own children. That continuity is the whole point of a family practice."

Through all of it, one thing has not changed. Bay Dental Associates has stayed independent and partner-owned, organized as Bay Dental Associates of Green Bay, Inc. It has never been part of a corporate dental chain, and the dentists who care for patients are the same people who own and run the practice.

Common Questions

What new patients ask us first.

Straight answers to the questions Green Bay families ask before their first visit.

Is Bay Dental Associates part of a corporate dental chain?

No. Bay Dental Associates is an independent, partner-owned practice, organized as Bay Dental Associates of Green Bay, Inc. It has no affiliation with any dental service organization or corporate dental chain. The dentists who care for patients are the same people who own and run the practice.

Is Bay Dental Associates the same as "Dental Associates"?

No. They are two different practices. Bay Dental Associates is a small independent, partner-owned family practice at 2460 Finger Road on Green Bay's east side. Dental Associates is a separate, much larger multi-location Wisconsin dental group. The similar names cause frequent confusion, but the practices are unrelated.

How long has Bay Dental Associates been in Green Bay?

The practice traces to 1972 in downtown Green Bay and took the Bay Dental name when Dr. Thomas J. Nockerts and Dr. Richard Jensen joined in 1981 and 1983. It has provided family dentistry to the greater Green Bay area from its current Finger Road office since 1992.

Where is Bay Dental Associates located, and what are the hours?

Bay Dental Associates is at 2460 Finger Road, Green Bay, WI 54302, on the east side of Green Bay. The office is open Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday from 8:00 to 4:30, Tuesday from 11:00 to 6:00, and Friday from 7:00 to 12:00. Call (920) 469-8484 to schedule.

Is Bay Dental Associates accepting new patients?

Bay Dental Associates welcomes new patients of every age across the greater Green Bay area, from a child's first visit to the dental needs of parents and grandparents. Call (920) 469-8484 or use the new-patient request to ask about scheduling a first visit.

Do you see children as well as adults?
