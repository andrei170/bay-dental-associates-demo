Search Visibility Audit Prepared by AG1 Consulting
Bay Dental Associates · Green Bay, WI

34 years on Finger Road, invisible to AI search.

An independent partner-owned practice with roots back to 1972, getting absorbed into a larger group's search results. Everything we built for the audit is linked below.

What we found

The audit, in one number.

0 / 5

Bay Dental Associates surfaced in zero of five buyer-intent AI searches we ran (family dentist Green Bay, dentist near me Green Bay, dentist east Green Bay, independent dentist Green Bay, best dentist Green Bay WI). The much larger "Dental Associates" group it gets confused with took the top slots.

  • A name-confusion problem the structured data should solve. Bay Dental Associates is a small independent partner-owned practice. Dental Associates is a separate large multi-location group. Sharp Organization and LocalBusiness schema is how the independent practice claims its own identity.
  • The partner-owned heritage is not in structured form. Roots to 1972, on Finger Road since 1992, team members here 20, 30, even 40 years. That continuity is exactly what AI engines cite, and it lives only as website prose.
  • No Dentist or MedicalBusiness schema. The structured-data layer AI engines read before deciding who to cite is effectively empty.
